One safe route from advice to action

  1. Authorized inputUse only the selected notes or transcript. Exclude sensitive or stale material.
  2. Workflow cardExtract the goal, inputs, steps, uncertainty, and stop conditions.
  3. Safe task testTry one reversible browser step with a visible completion check.
  4. Owner decisionReview the evidence before any send, publish, purchase, submission, or account change.

Stop: If the input is unauthorized, the task is unclear, or the action has a real-world consequence without an owner ready to approve it.
