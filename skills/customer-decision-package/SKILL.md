# Customer Decision Package

Install this package when the work in front of you is a customer or offer decision: what to improve, test, explain more clearly, or leave alone. It gives the AI a sequence for organizing evidence before the owner decides.

## What this package installs

Install only these three skills into the active local skills directory. Download the public `SKILL.md` source for each. Do not delete, move, rename, or overwrite unrelated skills.

| Skill | Job in the package | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `call-decision-system` | Turns one authorized call or a bounded transcript set into a Call Decision Brief with direct statements, uncertainty, inferences, and a review queue. | `https://why57.com/skills/call-decision-system/SKILL.md` |
| `feedback-prioritization` | Maps approved feedback into evidence-backed themes, frequency limits, uncertainty, and a keep/watch/test/decline recommendation. | `https://why57.com/skills/feedback-prioritization/SKILL.md` |
| `proof-library` | Creates source-linked claim cards when the owner needs to explain a chosen decision externally without overstating proof. | `https://why57.com/skills/proof-library/SKILL.md` |

Confirm the installed skill names and paths. If the environment cannot download or save skills, say so plainly and stop. Do not run any skill during installation.

## How the skills work together

1. Use `call-decision-system` for the specific customer conversation. It preserves direct statements, transcript uncertainty, and owner decisions instead of inventing agreement or action items.
2. Use `feedback-prioritization` on a bounded, authorized feedback set to test whether the call’s issue is repeated, one-off, or still uncertain. It returns a Feedback Map and one decision the owner needs to make.
3. If the owner chooses a message, sales response, capability update, or small experiment, use `proof-library` to identify what can be claimed and what still needs evidence.
4. Stop with a reviewable decision packet. The owner decides what to build, test, draft, record, or leave alone.

## First use

Ask for one decision to inform, the selected call transcript or notes, a bounded and authorized feedback set, the relevant date range, source labels, product constraints, and the named owner who will review the result. Do not ask for a full call archive, credentials, private account access, or permission to change a roadmap.

The useful end state is a decision that shows its evidence and uncertainty—not a false sense of customer consensus.

## Boundary

The AI may sort evidence, preserve contrary evidence, name unknowns, and propose a bounded test. The owner verifies the facts, chooses the decision, and authorizes any follow-on draft or work. Do not update a CRM, create tickets, contact customers, publish, schedule, or make product changes.
