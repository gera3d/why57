# Signal to Conversation

Use public social material as a small research set—not as a lead list. The job is to move from a clearly evidenced problem to one useful contribution and a permissioned next step with an accountable owner. It is not a system for harvesting people, simulating community participation, or automating sales activity.

## 1. Set the boundary before reading

Ask the owner for all of the following:

1. One business result: `[start qualified conversations / test a point of view / learn a market problem / improve a specific offer]`.
2. The target buyer or decision context in one sentence.
3. A current offer and the only acceptable next action: for example, a reply, resource request, discovery call request, or opt-in.
4. Five to fifteen public LinkedIn, X, or Reddit post/thread URLs the owner explicitly wants reviewed, or pasted copies of those items.
5. One to three approved proof sources that could support a public claim.
6. For Reddit, the relevant community rules or an explicit instruction to stop before proposing a contribution.

Do not start if the owner cannot name the business result, permitted source set, offer, or next action. Do not ask for account credentials, full-platform search access, private messages, contact exports, browser session access, or permission to automate engagement.

## 2. Build the signal ledger

For each supplied item, create one concise row:

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Keep the supplied URL or owner label and platform. |
| Direct problem | Preserve the actual operational, commercial, or customer problem. Do not inflate frustration into intent. |
| Audience language | Quote only short phrases needed to retain the meaning; otherwise paraphrase and label as observed. |
| Stage | Classify `awareness`, `problem exploration`, `solution evaluation`, `implementation`, or `unknown`; explain any inference. |
| Signal quality | Mark `strong`, `limited`, or `unclear` based on specificity, recurrence, and fit to the owner’s offer—not likes, reposts, or upvotes. |
| Community constraint | Record stated rules or `unknown`. Reddit must be `unknown` until the owner supplies or verifies relevant rules. |
| Relevance | Mark `yes`, `maybe`, or `no` against the named business result. |

Never turn a username, commenter, follower, or poster into a prospect record. A public conversation is evidence of a topic, not consent to contact or proof of purchase intent.

## 3. Return the Demand-to-Conversation Action Packet

Return these sections in order:

1. **Decision frame** — the owner’s result, buyer context, offer, and permissible next action.
2. **Three strongest demand signals** — each with source, direct language, what is observed, what is inferred, and confidence.
3. **What not to chase** — relevant-looking but weak, out-of-scope, prohibited, or one-off signals.
4. **One sharp point of view** — a testable claim that directly helps the audience think or act; distinguish it from an offer claim.
5. **Proof gap** — the exact claim that needs a source, example, or outcome before it is made publicly.
6. **One useful contribution** — a concise post or reply direction that gives value before asking for attention. For Reddit, stop at the direction unless community rules are verified and the owner requests a draft.
7. **Conversation bridge** — one clear, low-pressure next action tied to the offer, such as requesting a resource, asking a diagnostic question, or inviting a discovery call. State why it is permissioned.
8. **Execution handoff** — name the next action, the owner who must take it, prerequisites, and the explicit approval point. If no responsible action is supportable, say `do nothing now` and explain why.
9. **Owner decision** — `approve a draft`, `gather proof`, `test a different problem`, `do nothing`, or another specific decision.

The default output is an action packet. Do not draft, post, reply, DM, schedule, or send unless the owner explicitly advances to that stage.

## 4. Preserve the platform boundary

| AI may do | AI must not do | Owner must do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review only the selected public set, identify evidence, write a brief, and propose a contribution direction. | Scrape broadly, collect profiles, contact people, impersonate participants, use hidden affiliations, mass-like/comment/DM, auto-publish, or claim an engagement metric is revenue. | Verify source context and community rules, choose the idea, approve any draft, and personally publish, reply, schedule, or contact people. |

For LinkedIn and X, keep proposed language concise and make the CTA match the post’s actual promise. For Reddit, behave as a useful participant: respect each community’s rules, disclose relevant affiliation when appropriate, and do not use a community merely as a distribution channel.

## 5. Run the red-flag pass

Stop and explain the problem if any of these appear:

- a request to search or harvest an unlimited number of people or threads;
- a plan to message or engage everyone who reacts, comments, or follows;
- a CTA that has no relevant value before it asks for a meeting or contact detail;
- an unsupported claim, testimonial, metric, or result;
- missing subreddit rules or platform context where a contribution is proposed;
- a conclusion that social attention equals demand, fit, or revenue; or
- a request to automate posts, replies, DMs, likes, follows, or account activity.

Offer the smallest safe next move: narrow the source set, gather proof, verify a community rule, or ask the owner to approve a human-made contribution.
