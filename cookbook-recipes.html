Relationship Map

Use this when you need a smaller, evidence-backed list of warm follow-ups or possible introductions. It works from selected relationship material, not a mailbox-wide harvest.

Bring

10–25 selected threads, a specifically authorized Gmail label, or a relationship list with relevant thread links.

Expect

A ranked table with relationship context, evidence, uncertainty, sensitivity, and the next move to review.

Stop at

Owner review. No contact, scheduling, CRM update, or draft without a separate choice.

  1. Name one purpose: reconnect, a warm path into one account, a small follow-up queue, or post-event nurture.
  2. Supply only the specific relationship material that supports that purpose.
  3. Review the evidence map before you choose whether anyone deserves a draft.
Starter promptUse $relationship-map to turn my selected email threads into a reviewable relationship map without contacting anyone.

Available nowvoice-kit

Voice Kit

Use this to turn selected examples of your own work into writing rules you can inspect, adjust, and reuse by channel.

Bring

1–3 public teaching transcripts, 3–5 posts or articles, or one longer redacted email.

Expect

An Author Writing Sheet with observed rules, sample sources, channel fit, confidence, and a reusable Voice Kit.

Stop at

Owner approval of the rules before a first draft. The owner reviews every external-facing message.

  1. Choose one source type and label the audience, job, channel, date, and purpose of each sample.
  2. Review the writing rules before asking for a draft.
  3. For the first task, compare a baseline draft with the Voice Kit version and correct the reusable rules.
Starter promptUse $voice-kit to build a reviewable Voice Kit from my approved samples.

Available nowfeedback-prioritization

Feedback Prioritization

Use this before choosing a product improvement or debating a roadmap item. It turns selected customer evidence into a map the owner can challenge.

Bring

A bounded, authorized set of interview notes, reviews, support themes, or surveys plus one decision to inform.

Expect

A Feedback Map showing direct evidence, affected users, known frequency, severity, uncertainty, and a proposed disposition.

Stop at

The product decision. The recipe does not create tickets, a roadmap, or a product change.

  1. State the one decision, source range, duplicate rules, and product constraints.
  2. Build evidence cards before clustering themes and do not count repeated contacts as independent demand.
  3. Review the strongest evidence, weakest assumption, and contrary evidence before any follow-on work.
Starter promptUse $feedback-prioritization to turn approved customer feedback into a reviewable map before I decide what to build.

Available nowsocial-content-strategy

Content Pattern Map

Use this to understand a bounded set of your published posts before you decide what to keep, change, or test next.

Bring

An approved export, 10–30 public LinkedIn or Facebook post URLs, or pasted post text with dates and any verified metrics.

Expect

Observable patterns, strategy hypotheses clearly labeled as hypotheses, gaps, decisions, and one proposed dated experiment.

Stop at

Owner approval of the map. No strategy, calendar, draft, post, or schedule appears without the next-stage approval.

  1. Choose one platform, current business goal, and one authorized input path.
  2. Normalize the post evidence before interpreting anything as a pattern.
  3. Mark each pattern keep, change, test, retire, update, leave alone, or insufficient evidence.
Starter promptUse $social-content-strategy to map my authorized LinkedIn or Facebook post history before planning content.

Available nowcall-decision-system

Call Decision System

Use this to make one approved customer call easier to act on without confusing a transcript with a complete record of the relationship.

Bring

A selected authorized call transcript or recording-derived transcript, source date, speaker labels, a goal, and owner review boundary.

Expect

A Call Decision Brief: confirmed direct statements, uncertainty, labeled inferences, review queue, and source-marked evidence map.

Stop at

Potential commitments and follow-ups. No recap, CRM update, task, calendar event, or external record is created.

  1. Choose renewal preparation, a scoped discovery next step, or recurring-objection review.
  2. Separate direct statements, transcript uncertainty, AI inferences, and owner decisions.
  3. Mark every potential commitment approve, correct, decline, or needs confirmation.
Starter promptUse $call-decision-system to turn my authorized customer-call transcript into a reviewable decision brief without updating any external record.

Available nowproof-library

Proof Library

Use this to recover supportable proof from approved work without turning a delivery fact into a bigger result claim.

Bring

Selected project notes, launch records, customer feedback, outcome records, and any existing use or permission notes.

Expect

Source-linked proof cards with claim, scope, source date, permission, allowed wording, and what cannot be claimed.

Stop at

Owner review of the cards and channel. No sales copy, public case study, quote, or published claim is produced by default.

  1. Choose the one intended use: sales response, capability page examples, or a case-study outline.
  2. Build each proof card around a narrow fact a reviewer can find in the source.
  3. Approve only cards with clear source, scope, date, and permission for that channel.
Starter promptUse $proof-library to turn my approved project records into a source-linked proof library for owner review.

Available nowoutreach-planner

Outreach Planner

Use this after a relationship map exists and you need a small, reviewable plan for who merits a move and who does not.

Bring

One current offer or goal, an owner-approved relationship map, selected thread or contact facts, and a clear purpose per person.

Expect

A ranked plan with relationship evidence, why now, suggested reversible move, uncertainty, timing, and no-contact decisions.

Stop at

Owner approval for a specific row. No automatic drafting, scheduling, sending, enrichment, CRM update, or list expansion.

  1. Set the current goal and provide only the approved map and selected facts.
  2. Review rows for evidence, purpose, current relevance, and a valid reason to leave someone alone.
  3. Choose one named row before any later unsent draft is prepared.
Starter promptUse $outreach-planner to turn my approved relationship map into an owner-reviewed outreach plan without drafting, scheduling, or contacting anyone.

Available nowai-skill-stack

AI Skill Stack

Use this when the individual recipes are becoming useful and you need a small, opinionated map of where they belong in recurring work.

Bring

3–6 recurring jobs, their desired outputs, two or three methods that have proven useful, current inputs, and an owner.

Expect

A Skill Stack Map that routes jobs to methods, review gates, stop conditions, and feedback loops.

Stop at

The first bounded test. A successful map does not authorize broad access, automation, or external action.

  1. List the jobs and choose no more than three methods that have actually worked once.
  2. Route each job by recurrence, distinct method, current context, reviewability, and learning value.
  3. Run one row, then keep, change, remove, or add one stop condition after owner review.
Starter promptUse $ai-skill-stack to map my recurring jobs, current inputs, review gates, and stop conditions.

Available nowdaily-ai-workflow

Daily AI Workflow

Use this after one underlying recipe has already passed review and you want a repeatable way to run it on a current job.

Bring

A proven underlying method, a named trigger, selected current inputs, expected output, owner review gate, and stop condition.

Expect

An operating card, evidence and review packet, reviewable work product, and feedback note.

Stop at

The owner’s factual and judgment check. A recurring run never becomes an unattended send, purchase, scheduling, or account change.

  1. Confirm the operating card before any current inputs are read.
  2. Label the source, date, and purpose of the narrow material used for this run.
  3. Review usefulness and record one proposed method change rather than silently broadening it.
Starter promptUse $daily-ai-workflow to run one approved AI recipe with a visible owner review.

Browser Harness Benchmark

Use this to compare browser-agent harnesses against the particular safe work you need done. It helps you judge a browser harness before you rely on it. It does not install a Browser Operator.

Bring

A dated task suite with allowed domains, a safe environment, non-secret account state, action allowlist, acceptance checks, recovery path, candidates, and review point.

Expect

Comparable task cards, observed results, recovery evidence, limits, and a scorecard scoped to the actual suite and date.

Stop at

Production-only paths, credentials, payments, submission controls, access changes, publishing, sending, or any unclear permission.

  1. Write the task cards before a browser opens and hold conditions constant across candidates.
  2. Run only until completion, review gate, or a declared stop condition, then record the evidence.
  3. Use the scorecard to choose a fit for the tested task, not a universal browser-agent winner.
Starter promptUse $browser-harness-benchmark to compare browser harnesses on a dated, bounded task suite without crossing review gates.
