The Multi-LLM Context Engine

Written by: Gera YereminSeries: Agentic Automation

So, you recorded your mentorship call. You have a massive 12,000-word transcript sitting on your hard drive detailing exactly how to run your business outbound or configure your new SaaS architecture.

What do most people do? They paste it into ChatGPT and ask: "Summarize this."

And the AI spits out a generic bulleted list that gives you zero ability to actually execute the task. A summary is not a strategy.

Building the Harness

Instead of a single query, you need an AI Harness. I maintain subscriptions to OpenAI, Google, and Claude. When I have a highly complex strategy transcript, I use these models in tandem. I leverage the best reasoning model to extract the data, and the best formatting model to structure it.

But the real secret is the prompt. We don't want a summary. We want a machine-readable Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) that an agent can execute.

The Core PromptCopy & Paste This

"You are my technical co-founder and Chief Operating Officer. Review this mentor call transcript. Extract every specific, actionable recommendation my mentor gave me regarding [Topic].

Do not give me a summary. Break each recommendation down into a step-by-step browser workflow. Output this workflow as a structured JSON object defining a 'Skill' that a browser-automation agent can read and execute."

The JSON Output: From Talk to Code

When you feed this prompt alongside your raw transcript, the AI stops talking to you like a human and starts talking to your browser like an engineer.

Here is what the output looks like. This is the exact JSON structure we extract to trigger agentic automation for a government contract follow-up strategy:

{
  "skill_name": "Government_Contract_Outreach",
  "trigger": "New RFP Detected",
  "browser_actions": [
    {"action": "navigate", "target": "gmail.com"},
    {"action": "extract_rfp_data", "context": "mentor_guidelines_v2"},
    {"action": "draft_email", "tone": "authoritative_expert"},
    {"action": "schedule_send", "time": "tomorrow_0800_EST"}
  ]
}

Every time I trigger this new skill moving forward, the AI knows exactly what tone of voice to use, where to look for data, and how to format the email. I am building a library of high-level business skills based on expert advice.

But why go through the effort of turning the transcript into this JSON file? Why not just pass the raw mentor transcript to the AI every single time you need to do the task?

Because passing a massive transcript every time you need to click a button is the fastest way to burn your API budget. We need to talk about Token Economics.
