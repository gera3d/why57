The Mentorship Goldmine (and why you are wasting it)

Written by: Gera YereminSeries: Agentic Automation

Here is a hard truth about business coaching: most founders pay thousands for a mentor, take a bunch of notes, and then execute exactly none of it.

The gap between getting good advice and actually doing the work is where most businesses stall out. You get off a strategy call with a list of 15 things you need to change in your CRM, 5 new outreach campaigns to build, and 3 software tools to configure. It's overwhelming. So, it sits in a Google Doc and dies.

I stopped trying to do it manually. The shift happens when you stop taking notes and start capturing execution data.

The Knowledge-Execution Gap

If you look at the landscape of AI workflow automation right now, everyone is talking about using AI to generate things—code, copy, images. But generation isn't execution. The real breakthrough is Agentic Web Automation.

This is the shift from brittle, static scripts to AI agents that can "reason" about a user interface. They observe the browser state, decide on the next action (click, type, scroll), and navigate the web like a human.

Core Mindset Shift

Stop taking notes. Start recording the transcript.

To build an automated business, your mentor calls are no longer just conversations. They are the raw data inputs for your execution engine. Using tools like Fireflies, Fathom, or Otter to capture the exact transcript is mandatory.

How to Extract Procedural Value

When you get on a call with an expert, do not let them just give you high-level philosophy. If you want an AI to execute their advice later, you must ask procedural questions.

Instead of: "How should I do outbound?"

Ask: "If you were sitting at my computer right now, what exact buttons would you click? Where do you source the leads? What fields are you looking at in the CRM before you send the email?"

By forcing the expert to explain the physical, step-by-step clicks, you are generating a perfect transcript for an AI harness to read later.

The Era of Agentic Execution

By connecting the context of a highly specific, procedural strategy call directly to an agentic browser, you completely eliminate the "Execution Gap." The AI isn't just giving you a to-do list; it is clicking the buttons.

But how do we get the AI to understand that transcript? That is where the Multi-LLM Context Engine comes in.
