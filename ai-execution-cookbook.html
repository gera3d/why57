How it works

Give your AI the method. Keep the call.

The skill breakdown explains the job, the execution model, and the research behind it. The skill gives the AI the reusable method. You add the current facts and decide whether the output is useful enough to use.

01 · NAME THE JOB

Start with one useful piece of work.

Voice Kit starts with the email, post, or explanation in front of you. It does not ask the AI to “make your business better.”

02 · BRING A REAL SOURCE

Give it something to ground the work in.

Use a talk, post, email, or transcript you own or are allowed to use. The skill uses the sample and current job together.

03 · INSPECT THE WORK

See the draft and the choices behind it.

The AI drafts immediately and shows the observed patterns next to the output. You can see where the method helped and where it missed.

04 · CORRECT AND REUSE

Make the next draft better from feedback.

Your direct correction wins over an inferred pattern. Keep what reduces edits. Change what sounds wrong. Stop using any method that costs more judgment than it saves.

The AI can prepare. You authorize.

A CookBook skill can organize selected material, identify patterns, recommend, and draft. You still decide what is true, what gets sent or published, and what access the AI receives. That boundary is part of the method, not a disclaimer at the bottom.
