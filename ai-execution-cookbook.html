How guided AI execution works

Give your AI a business job it can carry forward.

Each recipe gives you the human guide. Each skill gives your AI reusable instructions for one job. The workstream supplies the current facts, so the output stays specific enough to review and useful enough to move.

01 · CHOOSE THE JOB

Start with one business outcome.

Name what must move now: a qualified conversation, a decision, a proof gap, a follow-up, or a delivery milestone. “Help with my business” is too broad to run.

02 · ADD THE RIGHT CONTEXT

Use only what belongs.

Supply selected records, source dates, constraints, and known gaps. Skip the archive dump and do not mistake attention for proof.

03 · REVIEW THE ACTION PACKET

See the move before you make it.

The skill returns a source-linked brief, recommendation, draft, or plan with uncertainty, the next action, its owner, and its approval point visible.

04 · APPROVE, LEARN, REPEAT

Let outcomes improve the skill.

The owner approves consequential action, records what happened, then keeps, changes, or stops the skill based on the result—not on activity alone.

The AI does the legwork. You commit the business.

The AI can organize evidence, research a permitted source set, classify, recommend, prepare an action packet, and draft. You still own sending, publishing, purchasing, submitting, signing, changing access, and representing the business. That is how the work stays accountable in the real world.
